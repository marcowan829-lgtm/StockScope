# Company icon sources

Bundled from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), commit `801eaab5b70f4933137069397ebbd2ce5f9ad56e`.
Only the icons used by StockScope's starter list are included. Individual SVG and brand-source URLs are recorded in `manifest.json`. Original icon paths are retained; the interface applies the library's brand color. McDonald's uses its golden mark on a red circle for contrast.

The library license and disclaimer are included beside these files. Company names and marks identify their respective securities. No affiliation is implied. Stocks without a bundled mark use a local initial badge; funds use a fund badge, and indices use numeric badges. The app makes no network requests to load icons.

Additional website icons were obtained from the relevant companies' public websites. Their exact asset URLs and official homepages are listed in `manifest.json`; image bytes are retained unchanged.
